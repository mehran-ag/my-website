My Education

PhD in Bioengineering

University of Auckland

Thesis: The Automated Construction and Verification of Physically Plausible Models of Physiological Systems

I completed my PhD at the University of Auckland, ranked 67th among universities worldwide. My research focused on developing an automated tool to check whether mathematical models of biological cells follow the rules of nature. Since many processes inside cells cannot yet be directly observed, researchers use available information to build models and simulate how cells behave. My work aimed to make checking these models easier through an automated tool. The tool is developed in Python programming language and is user-friendly for the best user experience and comfort.
